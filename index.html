How I Work

Philosophy

The name Rushd comes from the Arabic for sound judgment and intellectual maturity. These principles guide every engagement.

01

Change is a people problem, not a technology problem

Most transformation programmes fail not because the technology doesn't work, but because the people and processes around it weren't brought along. I start with stakeholders, incentives, and organisational readiness before touching a single tool.

02

Rigour over hype

AI is powerful. It is also oversold. I help organisations cut through the noise with honest feasibility assessments, grounded business cases, and a clear distinction between what AI can do today and what is still marketing.

03

Delivery is the only metric that matters

Strategy decks that sit on shelves are worthless. Every engagement I take on is oriented toward a tangible outcome: a system delivered, a process redesigned, a team upskilled, a decision made. If it doesn't ship, it doesn't count.

04

Ethics are not an afterthought

I'm completing an MA in AI Ethics because I believe governance, fairness, and accountability should be designed into transformation from the start. Not bolted on at the end to satisfy a compliance checklist.
